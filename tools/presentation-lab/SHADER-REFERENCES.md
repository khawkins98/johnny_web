# Further CRT shader comparisons

Primary-source review by the GPT-6 Astra adviser, checked 2026-10-23. “Impressive” separates tube-image reproduction, physical surroundings and motion; none is a universally calibrated CRT. Our current local lab implements an original VGA beam approximation and an actual public-domain CRT-Lottes reference. The candidates below are further comparisons, not already-integrated options.

These activity dates identify specific verified releases/changes. They do not assert absence of every later author build or repository change.

Preset packs are configurations, not additional rendering engines. **Sonkun** shared Guest-based samples on [August 11/13, 2026](https://forums.libretro.com/t/new-sonkun-crt-guest-advanced-hd-presets-thread/39091/1580), describing work toward another release. **CyberLab / Death To Pixels** provides extensive Mega Bezel/Royale/Megatron tuning; see the author's [February 2026 Megatron-v2 work](https://forums.libretro.com/t/sony-megatron-colour-video-monitor/36109/3067). Display-specific presets may not transfer well to a laptop; RGB variants are more relevant than composite-console styling for the proposed VGA target.

Modern Libretro `.slang` is generally Vulkan-oriented GLSL with framework bindings. The `.slangp` describes a pass graph, buffers/formats, sizes, LUTs and sampling. Neither is directly pasteable into a WebGL GLSL ES fragment host. The legacy `glsl-shaders` collection can be closer, but still needs host adaptation. [Libretro pipeline documentation](https://docs.libretro.com/development/shader/slang-shaders/).

License provenance must be checked per exact imported file/version, including include files and presets:

- Guest and Royale source headers declare **GPL-2.0-or-later**: [Guest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/libretro/slang-shaders/master/crt/shaders/guest/advanced/crt-guest-advanced.slang), [Royale](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/libretro/slang-shaders/master/crt/shaders/crt-royale/src/crt-royale-geometry-aa-last-pass.h).
- Mega Bezel's base project declares **GPLv3**; community packs can have independent terms.
- Beam Simulator declares **MIT**: [license](https://github.com/blurbusters/crt-beam-simulator/blob/main/LICENSE).
- A blanket license for all current primary **Megatron v2** files was not established in this review. Downstream MIT descriptions are insufficient provenance for importing an entire current pipeline.
- CyberLab's Mega Bezel preset pack has [custom restrictions](https://github.com/CyberLabSystems/CyberLab-Mega-Bezel-Death-To-Pixels-Shader-Preset-Pack/blob/main/LICENSE.md), including noncommercial/personal-modification and output-attribution conditions. Do not assume the shader's license also covers its preset pack.
- A definitive license for the latest Sonkun sample archive was not verified.

The recommended research order is **Guest RGB spatial comparison → Megatron v2 high-end reference → separate Beam Simulator motion experiment**. Mega Bezel's environment effects are a distinct presentation experiment. Our original VGA model and pinned public-domain Lottes integration remain available throughout. This ranking is an engineering judgment rather than a hardware-authenticity measurement.
