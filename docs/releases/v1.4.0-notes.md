# v1.4.0 — Island TV

> Unpublished draft. Proposed version and notes; PRs #84–#86 must land and the release plan must be completed before publication.

Johnny has a new home in the browser: a hand-painted castaway TV with coconut dials and a beachcomber's control shelf.

- **One Play button.** Start from the shelf or click/tap the original title picture. Options keeps everyday choices first and reveals advanced tuning when needed.
- **Working TV hardware.** Turn the upper dial through picture presets, use the lower dial for speed, and pause with the green button or Space. Walking, transitions, animation and sound pause together.
- **CRT picture choices.** Creative CRT, experimental VGA beam and Libretro CRT-Lottes, with remembered tuning and named Creative presets. Balanced CRT and Stretch to fill are the new defaults; saved preferences stay intact, and Original picture disables CRT.
- **Explore the island.** The Log provides day/gag navigation, current story position and expandable session statistics. Save a preview time as the current advancing story clock. Timeline playback honors the selected day/time.
- **Clear the furniture.** H hides/restores Island TV and uses half-step picture scaling. F fills the page with the screensaver without making the browser fullscreen.

The presentation lab and art-maintenance guide include research, source credits and original prompts. The original game buffers remain 640×480; CRT is a separate presentation effect, with a normal-picture fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
